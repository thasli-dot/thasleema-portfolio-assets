The Hardest Tradeoff

CSC-assisted verification vs. fully digital onboarding
Option AFully digital — faster to build, scales cheaper
Option BCSC phygital touchpoint — slower, real partnership overhead
Chose B because40% of target users are digitally illiterate. Digital-only verification excludes the users who need the platform most.
Proof it was right: +12% activation rate

North Star Metric

Total Monthly Successful Value Transactions (TMSVT)

Why not DAU? A Janani can open the app daily and never transact — that's optimizing for engagement theater.

Why not GMV? GMV misses the chit fund savings deposits that are the real lifeblood of nano entrepreneurs.

Why TMSVT? It captures commerce AND savings. If it's growing, Jananis are genuinely selling and saving more.

The Verification Leak

40% drop-off

At the verification stage — the single largest leak in the entire funnel, caused by fear of data theft and photo-upload difficulty. Everything else in the product is downstream of fixing this.

The fix: ID masking (number only, no document scan) + CSC-assisted offline touchpoint — result: +12% activation.

What I'd Do Differently

Validate before building. I assumed women would want digital chit fund records. The assumption held — but I'd test it with a 2-week paper prototype first.
Tighter metrics from day one. I designed 20+ KPIs, which shows deep understanding but not yet hard prioritization. A PM's job is "we track 5 things, not 25."
Talk to buyers, not just sellers. The whole product is Janani-centric, but transactions need two parties — demand-side insight might have changed verification design.
